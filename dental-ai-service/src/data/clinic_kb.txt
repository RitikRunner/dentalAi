# Stunning Dentistry Knowledge Base

## Payment & Insurance Policy
Stunning Dentistry does NOT accept direct insurance billing. Patients must pay out-of-pocket and claim reimbursement from their insurance provider using the invoice provided by the clinic. We accept cash, credit cards, and UPI payments. No EMI options are currently available.
