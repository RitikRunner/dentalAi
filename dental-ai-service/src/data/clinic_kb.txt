# Stunning Dentistry Knowledge Base

## Root Canal Treatment (RCT)
A Root Canal Treatment is a dental procedure to relieve pain caused by an infected or abscessed tooth. During the root canal process, the inflamed or infected pulp is removed and the inside of the tooth is carefully cleaned and disinfected, then filled and sealed. 
Pricing: Starts at Rs. 5,000 depending on the complexity of the tooth.
Recovery: Most patients recover fully within a few days. Mild discomfort is normal for the first 24-48 hours.

## Invisalign (Clear Aligners)
Invisalign is a modern alternative to traditional metal braces. It uses a series of custom-made, clear, removable aligners that gradually straighten teeth.
Process: The patient must first come in for a 3D scan consultation. Aligners must be worn for 20-22 hours a day.
Pricing: Pricing starts at Rs. 50,000, but a consultation is required for an exact quote.

## Teeth Whitening
We offer professional, laser-assisted teeth whitening that can lighten your teeth by up to 8 shades in a single 45-minute session.
Side Effects: Some patients may experience temporary tooth sensitivity for 1-2 days after the procedure.
